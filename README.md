# AstroSage Call Center Analytics

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing AstroSage call center operations to evaluate operational performance, customer satisfaction, astrologer performance, and service efficiency using Microsoft Excel.

The analysis explores call and chat interactions, customer ratings, call statuses, peak-hour activity, and operational improvement opportunities.

## Objectives
- Analyze call center performance and service outcomes.
- Compare customer satisfaction across Call, Chat, and Complementary services.
- Identify peak hours and operational bottlenecks.
- Evaluate astrologer performance using interaction volume, duration, and ratings.
- Identify risks and recommend improvements in training, technology, and operations.
- Explore a proposed budget allocation for operational improvements.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
- Excel formulas
- Data analysis and visualization
- KPI reporting

## Key Areas of Analysis
- Call and chat performance
- Call status and chat completion
- Customer ratings and satisfaction
- Astrologer performance analysis
- Hourly call volume and peak-hour analysis
- Operational risks and improvement opportunities
- Budget allocation analysis

## Key Insights
- Chat interactions showed a higher non-completion rate, highlighting an opportunity to improve chat engagement.
- Peak-hour analysis identified periods of high call demand and operational pressure.
- Customer ratings varied across service platforms, highlighting areas for service-quality improvement.
- Performance analysis helped identify differences in astrologer interaction volume, duration, and customer ratings.

## Recommendations
- Strengthen training and service quality improvement programs.
- Improve technology and platform reliability.
- Focus on peak-hour resource planning.
- Monitor customer satisfaction and service completion KPIs.
- Prioritize improvement initiatives through structured budget allocation.
